## Simple explanation

Support & Network is the team employees actually talk to when something isn't working.
They handle the first response to IT issues (tickets), keep people connected (Wi-Fi,
VPN, network access), and decide when a problem needs to be escalated to Infrastructure
or Applications.

## Technical explanation

Support & Network teams typically manage:

- **IT support / ticketing** — receiving, triaging, and resolving (or escalating)
  reported issues.
- **Troubleshooting** — diagnosing the cause of a reported problem.
- **Escalation** — passing a ticket to a more specialized team (Infrastructure,
  Applications) when first-line support can't resolve it.
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement)** — target timeframes for responding to and
  resolving tickets, usually based on priority/urgency.
- **Wi-Fi / LAN / WAN** — wireless access, local area networks (within a building/site),
  wide area networks (connecting sites together).
- **VPN** — secure remote connections into the company network.
- **DNS** — translates domain names into IP addresses.
- **DHCP** — automatically assigns IP addresses to devices on a network.
- **Routers / switches** — hardware directing traffic within and between networks.
- **IP addressing / connectivity** — how devices are identified and reach each other
  on a network.

## Why would a large company need it?

Thousands of employees generate a constant stream of IT issues — forgotten passwords,
broken Wi-Fi, printers offline, VPN failures. Without a dedicated first line of
support and functioning network infrastructure, employees can't work, and the
specialist teams (Infrastructure, Applications) would be flooded with issues that
don't need their level of expertise.

## Common responsibilities

- Answering and triaging incoming tickets
- Resolving common issues directly (password resets, Wi-Fi connectivity, printers)
- Escalating issues that need Infrastructure or Applications expertise
- Monitoring SLA compliance (response/resolution time targets)
- Maintaining network hardware and connectivity (routers, switches, VPN, Wi-Fi)
- Managing DNS/DHCP configuration

## Example problems

- An employee can't connect to Wi-Fi.
- An employee forgot their password.
- A user can't connect through VPN while working remotely.
- Several employees can't reach a particular server (could be network or server-side).
- A printer is offline.
- A user reports "the internet is slow" (could be Wi-Fi, WAN, or something else).
- A device gets no IP address (possible DHCP issue).
- A website/internal tool won't resolve (possible DNS issue).

## University connections

- **Networking** ↔ LAN/WAN/VPN/DNS/DHCP, routers/switches, IP addressing
- **Operating Systems** ↔ device-level connectivity troubleshooting
- **Secure Computing** ↔ VPN security, access control
- **Databases / SQL** ↔ ticketing systems store tickets in a database
- **Software Engineering** ↔ ticketing/escalation workflow design
- **Algorithms** ↔ SLA prioritization and routing logic
